# APEX OS v1.2.0 — Focused Admissions Portfolio

## Scope
- 13 university and university-specific scholarship profiles.
- 5 government scholarship routes.
- Official institution, government and programme-administrator links.
- Fall 2028 / AY 2028–29 date-status information.
- Previous-cycle references are isolated and labelled reference only.
- Türkiye’s published general annual January 10–February 20 window is labelled recurring, not a confirmed 2028 call.
- MEXT and GKS programme-calendar caveats are displayed on their profiles.
- Personal application status, priorities and profile notes.
- Local save, Supabase Auth, RLS-protected cloud snapshots, Realtime, JSON and CSV backups.
- JEE OS-style local server, Windows launcher, Capacitor and GitHub Actions architecture.

## Date integrity
Checked against official sources on 16 November 2026. No cycle-specific Fall 2028 / AY 2028–29 dates were found on the official pages checked. The app does not infer 2028 dates from previous cycles.
